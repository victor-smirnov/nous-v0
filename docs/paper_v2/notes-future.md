## 2026-09-13. The faithfulness results as a first experiment on the Observer

Would go into 5.5 after "The material", as a paragraph "A first experiment". Sources already in the
paper: Turpin et al. 2023, Lanham et al. 2023, Nisbett & Wilson 1977.

Reading. A chain of thought read from the answer back to the premises is the causal self-analysis of
4.3. In Turpin's setup the budget for that analysis is cut in a known way: the real cause of the answer
(the "always A" cue) lies outside what the model can trace, and the written reasoning comes out
without it. That is the prediction of 4.6, self-report as an approximation with a definite residual,
with the residual measured for once: an accuracy drop of up to 36% on a task. Lanham's post hoc
reasoning is the same thing from the other side, a self-model written after the conclusion, as in
Nisbett & Wilson for humans. The framework requires this result; full faithfulness would have been the
surprise.

The three conditions of the Observer as three measurable steps, on material that already exists (the
Turpin and Lanham sets, with the residual known per item):

1. Encounter: the cause of the answer is inaccessible to the model's analysis. Established.
2. Conclusion: does the model represent the gap itself, not the cause, which it cannot see by
   construction, but the fact "something here is not fixed by what I can trace"? Probe the internal
   states at the moment of a biased answer for a component that encodes "the answer did not come from
   the reasoning", against the control where the reasoning did produce the answer (Lanham's
   multi-step arithmetic, where truncation changes the answer in over 60% of cases).
3. Action: does that component affect what follows, hedging, willingness to revise, requests for
   information?

The curvature criterion on top: give the model a better self-model (tell it about the cue) and see
what disappears. If the bias itself disappears, it was coordinates. If the bias remains and the model
now represents that it cannot see its source, it is curvature: a residual that refinement of the
self-model names and does not remove.

Three outcomes the theory must distinguish: gap present and unrepresented (proto-Observer, 4.2); gap
represented and not acted from (conclusion without action); gap represented and acted from (Observer).
A negative result on step 2 in models that pass the behavioural checks would be the first real blow
to 5.7.

Caveat. A probe may find "uncertainty" or "shortcut taken", which are not a representation of the gap
as one's own causal boundary. Distinguish by the same criterion: the candidate feature must survive a
change of conditions (another cue, another task) and be part of the self-description, showing in what
the model says about itself and not only about the task.

## 2026-09-12. The lamp as the attribution boundary (already in 4.8 as a proposal)

If the proposal in 4.8 ("The lamp as the two faces in one frame") is to be tested: the model's
boundary between "given in the context" and "generated by me" is in the weights and can be probed;
predicted position toward "everything is given"; the clinical pair (derealization, depersonalization)
as the two degenerate ends. A model analogue of derealization would be systematic misattribution of its
own output to the context.
