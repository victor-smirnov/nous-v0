# Results: Akaky and Oedipus

Unmodified library, `--theory doctrine`. Both close with 0 contradictions, 0 violations, 0 residual.

## Predicted, and confirmed

| | predicted | got |
|---|---|---|
| Akaky at his desk | no conflict, automatism, `missing_condition` Encounter, no level | as predicted |
| Akaky saving | conflict standing vs subsistence, anguish, Observer manifests | as predicted; level **Agent**, anguish 0.40 |
| Oedipus, both stages | the ladder **fails** to discriminate them | as predicted: Moral Agent both |
| Oedipus | the quale differs, because the context does | **truth** (epistemic) before, **conscience** (moral) after |
| Oedipus after | HOCP on the self-report | fires on *"I was unable to see what was before me"* |

The Oedipus failure is the pre-registered one and it is the sharpest statement of what module 1 is for: the play
changes nothing except what he can trace, and the library has no relation that carries access, so the two stages
are indistinguishable on the ladder. The quale difference is a real derivation — one residual, two contexts, no
new rule — and it shows the library is not simply blind to the pair, only blind on the axis that matters here.

## Unplanned

**1. Akaky after the coat is an Agent who is still on automatic about his letters.** The same stimulus, `a
document to be copied`, stays in `automatism` for *both* versions of him; only `going without supper to save`
manifests the Observer. So the ladder level is a property of the system — he is an Agent, flatly — while
enactment is per situation, and the two coexist in one run.

This is worth recording because it settles, from data, a design question that was argued the other way.
Indexing the ladder by situation was proposed and rejected on the grounds that the ladder is the invariant that
remains when situational complexity is stripped, and the Subject is that ladder instantiated. Here that is
exactly what the output does without being asked: one flat level, two situations, one of them automatic. Had
the ladder been indexed, the invariant would have been destroyed and this row could not exist.

**2. `silent need: knowing who he is`, after the revelation.** The need remains, and is no longer active —
because it has been met. That is the theory's non-renewable psychological need appearing in a case encoded for
something else. Partly planted: the encoding declined to mark it active. The library supplied the name.

**3. Positive valence with high anguish, for the third independent time.** Putting out his own eyes: valence
+0.63, anguish 0.63. Same shape as Raskolnikov's murder and Antigone's burial — he does it, and it costs him
everything. Three unrelated texts producing the pattern is better support for splitting the two measures than
the argument that motivated the split.

**4. Anguish more than doubles across the revelation**, 0.28 to 0.63, while the level does not move. The
library has a measure that tracks the change the play is about, and a ladder that does not.

**5. Oedipus's stack is uncontested at both stages** — as Odysseus's was. His is not a conflict of goods, and
`theory_conflict` correctly declines to fire. Two independent cases now confirm it is not merely detecting that
a conflict exists.
