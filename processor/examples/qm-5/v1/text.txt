Some things are tiny, tinier than ants. Tiny things play a funny game.

Looking at it is how we find out. Like peeking in hide and seek!

Tiny things are like a spinning coin. Heads? Tails? Both at once until you look!

Looking at it tells you: heads or tails. But you cannot know which until you look.

Peek, and looking changes it! It was both at once; now it's only one. Tails!

Spin again, look again, lots of times. You cannot know which until you look. Count them!

Heads, tails, tails, heads... the pattern from many tries shows up: about half and half!
