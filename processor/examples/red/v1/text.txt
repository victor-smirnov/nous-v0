Look at the apple. Red. I know what red is: light of one wavelength bouncing off the skin and into my eye.

And in the eye, cells fire. Signals run to the brain, and somewhere in there a pattern is red. That part I can follow.

But none of that is what I see. What I see is this: the look of it. A wavelength does not look like anything.

Why does this pattern come with this look, and not the look of green, or no look at all? The story stops exactly there.

Maybe your red looks the way my green looks. You would still call the apple red. We would never find out.

Try to say what the look is like. I cannot. I can only point: that. Like that. Red.

Now I notice I am thinking about the look, and the thinking has a look too. Every step I take stands inside the thing I am asking about.

So I am left with two things, both true: the whole mechanism, and this. And no path from one to the other.

Fine. The apple is still red. I pick it up.
