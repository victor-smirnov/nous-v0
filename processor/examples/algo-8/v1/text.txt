When you brush your teeth, you do things in order: wet the brush, add paste, brush, rinse. Steps, one after another.

Now tell a robot: make a sandwich. It stares. You must say every step exactly: take bread, spread butter, add cheese.

Say "a bit of butter" and the silly robot smears the whole tub. Exact steps, same steps: same result every time.

Steps one after another must also end. "Spread butter, spread butter, spread butter" forever? No lunch, ever. There has to be a last step.

Best part: if every step is exact, anyone can follow it without knowing why. Your little brother could make it. Even the robot.

Exact steps, one after another, that come to an end, have a name: an algorithm. Your sandwich steps are an algorithm!

A computer is like that robot: it follows without knowing why. It only follows algorithms, exactly what the steps say, nothing more.

If one step in the algorithm is missing or mixed up, same steps, same result every time, the silly one. That's a bug.

You try it: pick something you do. Steps one after another, every step exact, and it comes to an end. Your own algorithm.
